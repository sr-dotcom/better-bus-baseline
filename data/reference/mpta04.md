## What was compared

Text extracted with `pdftotext -layout`. Section numbers are the same as in MTC-06.

- Section 3.7 (Service Frequency and Span) reads the same as the MTC-06 text quoted in `mtc06.md`, word for word, including "will strive", the 15-minute and 30-minute figures, "a minimum of three trips in each peak direction", the 5:00 a.m. to 1:30 a.m. span and "Exceptions will be based on ridership and productivity." The only difference after whitespace is normalised is a line break inside "off-peak".
- Section 3.3 (On-Time Performance) has the same definitions of late and early and the same trigger ("80 percent or less over a course of two service changes"). It corrects the MTC-06 typo "andremedial" and adds a sentence on rail.
- Section 2.4 still lists the four bus categories; its "Common / Local Routes" wording on weekdays and weekends is unchanged.

The audit's thresholds, periods and clause quotes are therefore unchanged. The scope of the check is these three sections only; other sections of MPTA-04 were not compared. The audit keeps the name MTC-06 because that is the policy text the method was written against.
